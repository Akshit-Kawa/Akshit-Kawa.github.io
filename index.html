About me

Most data graduates have never run a business. I have.

I tracked weekly sales patterns, built reports that changed decisions on the floor, got promoted to Store Manager, and used the data to negotiate a pay raise. That's where "data-driven" stopped being a phrase and became something I actually did every week.

Now I'm an Analyst Intern at a strategy consultancy — building CRM pipelines, qualifying leads, and mapping markets. Looking for a full-time analyst role where business context matters as much as technical skill.
